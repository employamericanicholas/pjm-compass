    How this was built

    1. The plant list is every generator that EIA-860M (September 2026) places in the PJM balancing authority with a first operating date from January 2015, including the few that have since retired. Capacity uprates of existing units are left out. EIA tracks generators of 1 MW and up, so rooftop solar is not here.
    2. A plant is one EIA plant ID. The two tabs split plants at 5 MW of nameplate capacity; a plant of exactly 5 MW is on the larger tab. Units added at a plant that already ran before 2015 are tagged "added at a pre-2015 plant" and can be filtered out.
    3. Generation is EIA-923 monthly net generation through August 2026. Where a new unit shares a plant with older units of the same type, it is credited with its share of capacity for each month it was online.
    4. Owners of plants of 20 MW or more were researched against press releases, regulatory filings and project trackers, with a source link on each. Smaller plants show EIA's equity owner where reported, otherwise the operating entity. Ownership changes often; each record says when it was checked.
    5. Plants whose reported interconnecting utility sits outside PJM, mostly community solar in Ameren Illinois territory, are flagged on the map and in the plant detail, and drop out of the reconciliation.
